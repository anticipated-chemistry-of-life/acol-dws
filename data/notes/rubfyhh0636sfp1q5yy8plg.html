Assignment Update

For all features fff :

  1. fff is unknown (assigned to the unknown molecule)
    1. pick molecule MMM among candidates for that feature
    2. if MMM is free, do the update hU→Mh_{U\rightarrow M}hU→M​
    3. if MMM is taken, assign the feature that has molecule MMM to unknown and assign MMM to fff hUM→MUh_{UM\rightarrow MU}hUM→MU​
  2. if fff is known (assigned to a molecule)
    1. set to unknown with probability β\betaβ hM→Uh_{M\rightarrow U}hM→U​
    2. pick molecule MMM among candidates with probability 1−β1-\beta1−β
      1. if MMM is free, do the update hM→M′h_{M\rightarrow M'}hM→M′​
      2. if MMM is taken, propose to swap the two molecules. hMM′→M′Mh_{MM'\rightarrow M'M}hMM′→M′M​
